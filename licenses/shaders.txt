slot's screen shaders are ports of shaders from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders

LCD3x by Gigaherz. Public domain.

Dot by Themaister. Public domain.
Changes: sharper dots (falloff 4.0) and a 0.85 blend.

zfast_lcd_standard by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.

simpletex_lcd by jdgleaver, using the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
Changes: the grid bias is 1.0, so black pixels carry no grid.

Additional GBA shader presets (QA code101):
LCD Grid V2 by cgwg (libretro GPL-compatible shaders).
GBA Color by hunterk, modified by Pokefan531. Public domain.
LCD1x and Simpletex LCD + GBA Color by jdgleaver. GPLv2-or-later.
zfast-lcd by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42). GPLv2-or-later.
AGB-001 and AGS-001 by endrift. Mozilla Public License 2.0.
Sharp Bilinear by Themaister. Public domain.
Slot GLES2 ports of multi-pass shaders are approximations until pixel parity
is validated on KONKR. Preset parameter values are preserved.
Source: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/handheld

CRT shaders for the NES, Super Nintendo and Mega Drive, one pass each:
zfast_crt by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017. GPLv2-or-later.
crt-pi by davej, copyright (C) 2015-2016. GPLv2-or-later.
crt-gdv-mini(-ultra) by guest(r), copyright (C) 2017-2018, with Dr. Venom
and metallic77. GPLv2-or-later. Trinitron preset, no curvature.
CRT Geom Deluxe from the crt-geom family by cgwg and others. GPL.
No curvature.
fakelottes by hunterk, after crt-lottes by Timothy Lottes. No curvature.
crt-aperture by EasyMode. GPL.
Source: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/crt

More shaders for the NES, Super Nintendo and Mega Drive, one pass each,
preset defaults:
crt-easymode by EasyMode. GPL.
crt-hyllian by Hyllian, copyright (C) 2011-2020. MIT License.
SABR v3.0 by Joshua Street. GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
Super Eagle by the DOSBox Team, after Derek Liauw Kie Fa's 2xSaI. GPL.
Changes: its two passes (2x picture, then bilinear stretch) drawn as one,
colours compared exactly instead of as one packed number.
xBR-lv2 by Hyllian, copyright (C) 2011-2016. MIT License.
Source: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/crt, .../xbr,
.../sabr and .../eagle

Dot Matrix (Game Boy): Gameboy Color Dot Matrix v0.9 by LuigiRa, after
Harlequin's Gameboy shader. GNU General Public License version 3.
Its background texture is the shader's dmg-bg, scaled to 1024x1024.
Changes: its three passes drawn as one; the DMG preset's colours are the
player's Game Boy palette (its lightest shade behind, a dark one for the dots).
Source: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/handheld

Dither Blending (Mega Drive): Genesis Dithering and Pseudo Transparency
(GDAPT) v1.3 by Sp00kyFox, 2014, from the libretro shader collection.
Its two passes run on the picture before it is drawn, MODE 0, PWR 2, STEPS 1.
Used in slot. with the author's permission.
Source: https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders/tree/master/dithering

LCD Effect (Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance): Pixel Transparency
and Sunlight Shimmer v3.0, copyright (C) Matt Akins. Adapted for slot. with
the author's permission. Author defaults; its cache passes (shadow, film
noise, light field) are computed in place, in one pass after the LCD shader.
Source: https://github.com/mattakins/Pixel_Transparency

Simpletex paper texture: slot.'s own paper texture, drawn again since 2.2.
